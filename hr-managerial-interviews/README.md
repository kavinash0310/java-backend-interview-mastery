# Module 23 — HR and Managerial Interviews

> **Module guide:** Prepare clear, specific answers about your work, decisions, communication, and fit for a role.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain your project and contributions without losing the interviewer in detail.
- Use real examples to discuss incidents, trade-offs, and teamwork.
- Prioritize production issues and ask useful questions about the role.

## Topic coverage

- Project architecture and personal contribution
- Production incidents and lessons learned
- Motivation for a specific company and role
- Deadlines, prioritization, and disagreements
- Technical choices and trade-offs
- Questions for the interviewer
